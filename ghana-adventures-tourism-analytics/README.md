# Ghana Adventures — Turning Booking Data Into Demand, Retention & Revenue

**Type:** Independent case study
**Tools:** Power BI, Excel / data cleaning
**Prepared by:** Xorse Adjo, Data Analyst

## Problem

Ghana Adventures (tour operator) faced inconsistent seasonal bookings, low repeat customer rates, and a long tail of underperforming tour packages.

## Approach

- Cleaned and structured 249 bookings, fixing 25 missing/placeholder entries across price, season, and channel fields
- Modelled the data as relational tables (bookings, customers, packages) for Power BI
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing revenue by region, monthly visit trends, and revenue-visits correlation

## Key Findings

- **Demand is seasonal, not random** — Jan–Jun averages ~29 bookings/month (nearly 3x the Aug–Nov slow season)
- **A handful of tours carry the business** — 68% of packages (50 of 73) sold just one booking each
- **One destination dominates** — Mole National Park drives more than 2x the next-best destination
- **Repeat customers matter** — 37% of bookings are repeat customers; Private Tour bookings return 71% of the time vs 26% for Individual bookings and 13% for Group Tour

## Recommendations

- Rebalance the tour portfolio toward high performers (e.g. Safari Experience, Volta Adventure)
- Consolidate one-off, occasion-named listings into 4–5 seasonal campaigns
- Build a loyalty loop to convert first-time guests into repeat customers
- Use tiered, data-informed pricing and track results monthly in Power BI
